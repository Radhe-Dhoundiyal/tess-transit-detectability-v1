# Data sources — RC2

- NASA Exoplanet Archive `pscomppars`: planet, orbit, transit, stellar, and target metadata.
- TESS SPOC light-curve products obtained through MAST.
- First-party Phase 5B recovery, Phase 6A.2 injection-recovery, and Phase 6B modeling outputs.

RC2 contains no raw third-party FITS or Parquet products. Five catalogue
parameter classes lack provable row-level literature mappings in the retained
local metadata and are explicitly unresolved in
`docs/SOURCE_SPECIFIC_BIBLIOGRAPHY_V1_0_0_RC2.md`.

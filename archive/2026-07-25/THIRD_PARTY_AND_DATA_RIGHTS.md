# Third-party and data rights

The project licenses cover only first-party material identified in the license
coverage map. They do not apply to NASA, MAST, TESS, NASA Exoplanet Archive,
literature, upstream catalogues, raw/processed mission data, third-party
software, trademarks, logos, or mission identifiers. RC1 excludes third-party
raw downloads and uncertain-origin files. Factual hashes and provenance
metadata are included for verification without asserting ownership over their
referents.

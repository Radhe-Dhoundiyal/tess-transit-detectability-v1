# Acknowledgments

The light curves are NASA TESS observations obtained through MAST. Catalogue
metadata comes from the NASA Exoplanet Archive, operated at Caltech/IPAC
under NASA support. The TESS mission is funded by NASA's Science Mission
Directorate.

For data reuse, see the [Exoplanet Archive acknowledgment guidance](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/acknowledge.html),
[MAST publishing guidance](https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/) and
[mission acknowledgment wording](https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/mission-acknowledgements).
Original product identifiers and references are preserved. The five missing
catalogue-literature mappings are described in [limitations](docs/LIMITATIONS.md).

The search uses Astropy BoxLeastSquares; numerical and statistical work uses
NumPy, pandas and SciPy. Tests additionally use pytest and PyArrow. See the
[BLS documentation](https://docs.astropy.org/en/stable/timeseries/bls.html) and
[dependency notices](DEPENDENCY_NOTICES.md). No NASA endorsement is implied.
The saved analysis does not document use of Zooniverse or Planet Hunters
classifications.

I directed the research questions, experimental approach and interpretation
of results. ChatGPT and Codex assisted with software implementation,
mathematical suggestions, testing, code review and documentation. I am
responsible for the final work.

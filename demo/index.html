Method
Historical V1: selected TESS light curves and preprocessing → blind BLS candidate search → catalogue truth matching. A separate synthetic-injection branch tests recovery of known inserted signals. This interface reads those saved results; choosing a case changes the display only.
The observed endpoint is top-10 direct period-and-epoch recovery among eligible catalogue signals: 15/51. The intention-to-benchmark endpoint is 15/70, including 19 outside-range signals. Synthetic direct-or-harmonic recovery includes the same direct recoveries: 123/320, not 98 plus 123.
Limitations
Finite, selected benchmark; these rates do not describe all TESS planets or population completeness. Observed and synthetic outcomes are distinct. The WASP-127 b saved direct result carries an ambiguity flag. Its PRIMARY final_keep samples are a retained processed subset; the display does not rerun preprocessing.
Synthetic trials are clustered by host and use a fixed empirical central/circular profile. Model validation is internal. The former 50-count observed bar figure omitted the single alias; this interface retains all 51 eligible outcomes plus 19 outside range.

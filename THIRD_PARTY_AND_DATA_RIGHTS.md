# Third-party and data rights

The MIT and CC BY 4.0 choices cover first-party
software and original prose/analysis. They do not relicense NASA, TESS,
MAST or NASA Exoplanet Archive content, mission identifiers, trademarks,
factual external metadata, upstream observations, literature or third-party
software. The current `provenance/license_coverage.csv` distinguishes these
scopes; the historical coverage map remains archived.

Saved-record and curve-display files are mixed-origin analysis derivatives.
Preserve service attribution, product identifiers and data provenance.
Including factual values does not assert exclusive ownership. No mission
logos, third-party papers, proprietary assets, large raw mission files or
dependency distributions are included.

Official guidance for data attribution is linked in
[acknowledgments](ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md). It supports attribution requirements
and exclusions; it is not a legal certification of every future reuse.
The copyright notice covers first-party rights and does not extend to
upstream observations or third-party software. Applicable software assistance
is acknowledged in the repository credits. No DOI or academic publication
is claimed.
